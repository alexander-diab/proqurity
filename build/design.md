Use Case: 
"Finance Dep. of Vandenberg wants a report on compliance of change price events in Purchase orders"

Audit Trail / Workflow:
- We look at POitems where event "price change" occurs.
- We only look at POs, where the price change has been taken place later than 7 days of PO creation. All lower is considered as correction
- Is there a frame contract with the vendor. If not --> go to F
- Read the frame contract with conditions about price change
  - Can the price be changed anyway?
  - Threshold mentioned? Below threshold is ok or needs another condition?
  - Deadlines mentioned? must be announced? Other 
  - Other conditions?
- Find an email thread for the PO
  - Approved? by Whom? When?
  - Announced? When
- Approver has permission?
